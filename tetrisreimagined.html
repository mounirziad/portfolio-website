Tetris Reimagined

Tetris Reimagined is a personal C++ project where I rebuilt Tetris entirely from scratch — rewriting the grid, block rotation, collision, and scoring systems from the ground up. I wanted to take the timeless puzzle game and twist its physics to challenge how players think about space.

  • Gravity Shifter: Allows gravity to shift in any direction, sending blocks sideways or even upward.
  • Dynamic Blockades: Random obstacles appear on the grid, forcing on-the-fly strategy shifts.
  • Custom Color Palette: Clean modern look with smooth transitions and high-contrast visuals.
  • Pure C++ Logic: Utilizing the Raylib library and C++ — every movement, piece spawn, and rotation coded by hand.
